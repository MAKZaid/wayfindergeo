01The shift

The rules of search are being rewritten.

Buyers now ask an AI and get one short answer instead of ten links. If you aren’t named in that answer, you might as well be invisible.

  • The answer matters most. An assistant reads dozens of pages and writes one reply for the buyer. Being in that reply now matters more than ranking.
  • What others say counts. Reviews, Reddit threads and comparisons shape how models describe you, just like your site does.
  • Fewer visits, but better ones. People who arrive from an AI recommendation know why they came and tend to convert better.

07Questions

The things people ask before signing.

What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?

SEO earns you a position in a list of links. GEO earns you a mention inside a generated answer. The inputs overlap, since models still lean heavily on indexed content, but the targets are different. GEO cares about how easily a passage can be retrieved, how clearly models understand who you are, whether other sources back you up and how quotable your content is. We run both because neither works properly alone.

Can you actually influence what ChatGPT says about my brand?

Influence, yes. Control, no, and be skeptical of anyone promising otherwise. Models draw on indexed pages, live retrieval and the wider record of what the internet says about you. We work on all three. Your own pages become the cleanest source a model can find. The signals about who you are get clearer. And we build your presence on the sites models already pull from in your category.

Which AI engines do you track?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Grok. We also watch the sources those engines lean on, such as Reddit, YouTube and review sites, because a lot of recommendations start there.

Do I still need SEO?

Yes. AI engines still pull heavily from the search index, so a site that’s hard to crawl or rank is hard to cite. Foundational SEO comes with every plan.

What is in the free audit?

Send us your domain and we come back with a snapshot of your AI visibility: which engines mention you, the prompts where you’re missing and which competitors take the slot instead. It’s yours to keep, with no contract and no pitch deck.

How long before I see movement?

Technical and structural fixes register within weeks, and crawler activity is usually visible inside the first month. Citation share generally moves between month two and month four, depending on how competitive your category is and how much authority you start with. Anyone quoting you a fixed date is guessing.

How do you report on something this new?

Every plan gets a written read on what moved, what didn’t and what we’re changing next: once at the end of Compass, monthly on the retainers. North Star clients also get a live dashboard tracking visibility score, share of voice against named competitors, prompt-level wins and losses, citation sources and AI crawler activity on your pages.

How many prompts do you track?

Up to 50 priority prompts on Compass and Beacon, and up to 300 on North Star, across every major AI engine. We pick them with you from the questions your buyers actually ask.

Which plan should I pick?

Compass if you want the full picture and the fundamentals fixed once, without a retainer. Beacon if you want that work to keep going every month. North Star if you’re actively competing for the recommendation slot and want off-site work, content production and faster cycles. If you’re unsure, Compass is the low-commitment way to start, and nothing in it is wasted if you move to a monthly plan later.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No setup fee and no percentage of ad spend. Compass is a single payment with nothing after it. The monthly plans start with a 90-day term, since citation share usually takes two to four months to move, then run month to month.

What if no plan fits?

Multi-location businesses, ecommerce catalogs and enterprise scopes are quoted separately. Tell us on the call. If we’re not the right fit, we’ll say so.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly and we usually reply within one business day.
