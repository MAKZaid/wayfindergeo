The shift

Search did not slow down. It moved.

Answers are synthesized before the click now. The brands quoted inside those answers win the consideration set by default. Ranking is no longer the finish line. Being retrieved, cited and recommended is.

Models fan one prompt into dozens of sub queries, then stitch an answer together. If your brand is not inside it, you were never in the running, whatever position you hold.

Reviews, Reddit threads and comparison posts shape what models say about you. Off site coverage is now on page real estate.

Fewer sessions, higher intent. Someone arriving from an AI recommendation has already been pre sold, which is why they convert at a multiple of ordinary organic visits.

Questions

The things people ask before signing.

What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?

SEO earns you a position in a list of links. GEO earns you a mention inside a generated answer. The inputs overlap, since models still lean heavily on indexed content, but the optimization targets are different. GEO cares about passage level retrievability, entity clarity, third party corroboration and how quotable your content is. We run both because neither works properly alone.

Can you actually influence what ChatGPT says about my brand?

Influence, yes. Control, no, and be skeptical of anyone promising otherwise. Models draw on indexed pages, live retrieval and the wider corpus of what the internet says about you. We work on all three: making your own content the cleanest source available, strengthening entity signals so models know who you are, and building presence on the third party sources they already pull from in your category.

How long before I see movement?

Technical and structural fixes register within weeks, and crawler activity is usually visible inside the first month. Meaningful citation share generally moves between month two and month four, depending on how competitive your category is and how much authority you start with. Anyone quoting you a fixed date is guessing.

How do you report on something this new?

You get a live dashboard tracking visibility score, share of voice against named competitors, prompt level wins and losses, citation sources and AI crawler activity on your pages. Plus a written monthly read on what moved, what did not and what we are changing next.

Which plan should I pick?

Beacon if you need to establish presence and fix the fundamentals. North Star if you are actively competing for the recommendation slot and want off site work, content production and faster cycles. Most teams start on Beacon and move up once the baseline report shows what the gap costs them.
